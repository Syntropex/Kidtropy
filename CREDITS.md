# Credits

Kidtropy's code is MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Everything it uses is free and open:

## Voice

All spoken lines are pre-recorded with **[Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)** (Apache-2.0),
an open-source text-to-speech model, using the `af_heart` voice, via
[kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) (MIT).
The clips in `public/voice/` are generated by `scripts/voice/generate.py`.

## Pictures

- Most pictures are **[Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji)** 3D,
  © Microsoft Corporation, MIT License (see `public/pics/LICENSE-fluent-emoji.txt`).
- The excavator, bulldozer, dump truck, cement mixer, road roller, garbage truck, tow truck,
  monster truck, traffic cone, triceratops, stegosaurus, pterodactyl and fossil are original
  drawings made for Kidtropy (`src/art/art.ts`, MIT).

## Sounds

Animal and vehicle sounds come from [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org) and are all
**CC0 / public domain**. Thank you to every recordist. The full list with links is in
[`public/sounds/CREDITS.md`](public/sounds/CREDITS.md). They are fetched and trimmed by
`scripts/sounds/fetch.py`.

Short UI effects (pops, dings, fanfares) are synthesized in code (`src/lib/sfx.ts`).

## Music

The xylophone and drums are synthesized in code (`src/games/music-synth.ts`), so there are no
music files. The animal piano plays the CC0 animal sounds above at different pitches. Songs
are traditional, public-domain tunes (Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Mary Had a Little Lamb,
Old MacDonald, Row Row Row Your Boat, The Wheels on the Bus, Happy Birthday).
